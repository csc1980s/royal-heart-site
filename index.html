QUEST FOR THE

ROYAL HEART

A 16-bit platformer set inside a deck of cards. Slash, craft and draw poker hands from fallen foes to rescue the Queen of Hearts from the Joker.

GAMEPLAY

Recorded straight from the game: riding Rubywing over the volcano.

A KINGDOM OF CARDS

♥

Draw poker hands from your foes

Every enemy drops a card. Chain a pair, a straight or a full house mid-fight for big bonuses.

♠

Nine bosses. Then the Joker.

Kings, golems, a lava matriarch, a sea leviathan and an airship captain, each with telegraphed attacks you can learn and punish. Then the Joker's House of Cards.

♣

Ride a dragon. Joust a knight.

Hatch Rubywing and breathe fire across a volcano, then take the lance against the Jack of Spades in the Royal Tourney.

♦

Craft your way through

Gather timber, twine and stone. Build ladders, bridges and tools at card tables, and find the hidden Royal Suits in every stage.

The Fleet of Spades has raided the Kingdom of Hearts and carried off the Queen. Jack of Hearts sets out across seven card realms, from diamond caverns and clover forests to a frozen peak, a lava jungle, a sunken reef and an airship armada, to bring her home.

At the end of the road waits the Trickster Joker, in a House of Cards built for one last show.
